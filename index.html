About Jagadish Bhatta

I’m Jagadish Bhatta, a Data Analyst and Digital Marketer with a Bachelor's degree in Information Technology from Tribhuvan University. My work focuses on digital marketing, campaign analysis and standalone data analysis.

I help businesses connect marketing activities with measurable goals through Meta Ads, Google Ads, SEO, Email and WhatsApp marketing. By analysing traffic, engagement, leads and conversions, I aim to identify what works and recommend improvements.

My data analysis work includes cleaning and preparing data, extracting information with SQL, exploring datasets and creating clear reports using Python, Excel, Google Sheets and Tableau.
